# Pilot's Final Checklist

> Source: "Vibe Leading The AI: The Corporate Race Against Machines" (Jean Machuca). Framework concept: the Pilot's Final Checklist, five checkpoints with sub-checkboxes.

Run this end to end at project kickoff, quarter start, or after significant drift. Tick every box.

## 1. The Vibe Calibration

- [ ] The Mission Script is high-fidelity: poetic clarity a human feels.
- [ ] The Mission Script is high-fidelity: engineering precision an AI can execute.
- [ ] The Frequency is set: Stealth/Precision mode (correctness, no surprises).
- [ ] Or the Frequency is set: High-Velocity/Market-Capture mode (speed to insight).
- [ ] The Outcome field names a measurable definition of done.

## 2. The Garage Inspection

- [ ] MCP servers are live. (The Hybrid Data Engine, the Document Architect, the Real-Time Scout.)
- [ ] The Privacy Shield is active: Sanitization Gate tokens identity, the Vault keeps restricted PII on-premise.
- [ ] The HUD is visible to the Pilot.
- [ ] Observer Access is granted to the people who used to attend status meetings.

## 3. The Diamond Squad Roll Call

- [ ] The Ops Mechanic is in position (maintains the Garage of tools and pipelines).
- [ ] The Logic Navigator is in position (maps mission goals into the HUD).
- [ ] The Creative Pilot is in position (crafts Mission Scripts that coach the AI how to speak).
- [ ] The Risk Spotter is in position (watches for Hallucination Debt).
- [ ] The "Bridge" is dead: intent travels to agents via Mission Scripts, not translators.

## 4. The Double Tenaza Grip

- [ ] Ready to Let Go: the system is automated enough for the grip to loosen.
- [ ] Ready to Grip: telemetry triggers are set to alert the moment AI drifts.
- [ ] Grip 1 (Human Intuition) is active: I can feel when a project smells wrong.
- [ ] Grip 2 (Machine Precision) is trusted: I trust the HUD telemetry more than a feeling.

## 5. The Circuit Delta Target

- [ ] The Lap Time baseline is recorded.
- [ ] The Circuit Delta target is set (Lap times must decrease).
- [ ] Traction stays green while the target is chased (velocity without traction is a crash).

## Pilot's Oath

> I am no longer a facilitator of meetings. I am an orchestrator of intelligence. I do not manage by the clock; I lead by the Scoreboard. My technical authority is my engine; my poetic vision is my compass. I will not be a fossil. I am the Pilot.
