# Traction vs Velocity

> Source: "Vibe Leading The AI: The Corporate Race Against Machines" (Jean Machuca). Framework concept: velocity without traction is a spectacular way to crash.

## The example

- **Velocity:** "Our AI generated 1,000 lead-gen emails in 60 seconds."
- **Traction:** "Did those emails result in a 5% increase in booked meetings, or did they just get our domain blacklisted for spam?"

Velocity is the machine's throughput. Traction is the outcome the Vibe intended. A HUD with only velocity reads like a speedometer with no compass: fast, and pointed at the mountain.

## Auditing for drift

Drift is when output keeps shipping but stops moving the metric. Symptoms to look for:

1. **Volume without outcome.** Reports and emails and tickets produced, lead-gen and satisfaction numbers flat. The machine is doing its job; the Vibe is not being re-measured against the market.
2. **Muscle memory shipping.** The squad ships the same shape of work every Lap because it worked in Lap 3, while the environment changed in Lap 9. Check the Script: has the market moved?
3. **The metric became the idol.** A KPI that keeps getting met but no longer correlates with value is theater. Check the HUD: are metrics still measuring the right things?

## Sanity checks for the Pit Stop

- For every automation that "worked," ask: "Did it produce the outcome in the Vibe, or did it just produce output?"
- Compute the Traction for the Lap (phases scoring 8-10 on the IRA rubric divided by total phases) alongside raw volume. If volume rose and Traction fell, the Lap was fast and wrong.
- If the domain got blacklisted, the Vibe should have had a Constraint. Log it as a drift kill and sharpen the Script.

Velocity without Traction is the single most efficient way to crash an AI-assisted squad. The Pit Stop is where you catch it.
