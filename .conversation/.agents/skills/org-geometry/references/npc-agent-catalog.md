# NPC Agent Catalog

> Source: "Vibe Leading The AI: The Corporate Race Against Machines" (Jean Machuca). Framework concept: NPC Agents and how Specialists orchestrate them.

## The three canonical NPCs

### The Researcher NPC
- **Mission:** scours market data 24/7.
- **HUD feed:** the Real-Time Scout (Web & API Connector) pipes market shifts and competitor moves into the HUD.
- **Frequency:** High-Velocity/Market-Capture. Breadth and speed over polish.
- **Contract:** one question per pass. "Name the top three competitors that changed pricing this quarter," not "tell me about the market."
- **Audit:** every claim cites a source; unverifiable claims are flagged, not asserted.

### The Auditor NPC
- **Mission:** checks every line for Hallucination Debt.
- **HUD feed:** the output streams of the other NPCs and the specialists' passenger-facing artifacts.
- **Frequency:** Stealth/Precision. Its job is to say no.
- **Contract:** tallies debt points (1 per unverifiable claim, 2 per wrong-cited source, 3 per invented metric) and feeds the Risk Spotter.
- **Audit:** when the tally crosses the red band, the Risk Spotter snaps the connection on the offending agent.

### The Drafter NPC
- **Mission:** produces the first 80% of every document from the Pilot's Vibe.
- **HUD feed:** the Document Architect (Ecosystem Connector) for read/write on the corporate paper trail, aligned to the Mission Script.
- **Frequency:** High-Velocity for the first pass, then the human Specialist re-calibrates to Stealth/Precision for the final 20%.
- **Contract:** the Specialist writes a 5-minute Mission Script and the Drafter generates the report in 10 seconds.
- **Audit:** the Auditor NPC checks the draft; the Draft is not the Vibe until Traction is green.

## How Specialists orchestrate them

- **The Creative Pilot** writes the Mission Scripts. Without a sharp Script, the Researcher drifts into trivia and the Drafter freewheels into fiction.
- **The Ops Mechanic** keeps the feeds alive. A dead connector starves the Researcher; a leaky one poisons the Privacy Shield.
- **The Risk Spotter** owns the Auditor's outputs and compliance score. The Auditor flags; the Risk Spotter snaps.
- **The Logic Navigator** turns each NPC's contract into a HUD metric so the agents' output is scored like everything else.

One NPC, one contract, one feed, one audit. That is the entire orchestration pattern.
