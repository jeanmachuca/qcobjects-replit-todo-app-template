# Diamond Squad Roles

> Source: "Vibe Leading The AI: The Corporate Race Against Machines" (Jean Machuca). Framework concept: the 5-Person Racing Squad: 1 Pilot + 4 Specialist Crew + 100+ NPC Agents.

## The Pilot

- **Responsibilities:** sets the Vibe, writes the Mission Scripts, monitors the HUD, runs the Laps, owns the Double Tenaza.
- **Replaces:** the manager-as-facilitator. The Pilot does not facilitate meetings; the Pilot orchestrates intelligence.
- **Success signals:** Ciruit Delta shrinking while Traction stays green; the HUD visible; the Bridge dead.

## The Ops Mechanic

- **Responsibilities:** maintains the Garage of AI tools and data pipelines; keeps the MCP servers live (Hybrid Data Engine, Document Architect, Real-Time Scout); owns the Privacy Shield mechanics.
- **Replaces:** the infra/DevOps lead as far as agents are concerned, but scoped to keeping agents fed and gated.
- **Success signals:** connector uptime high; data latency low; no sensitive patterns leaking past the Sanitization Gate.

## The Logic Navigator

- **Responsibilities:** maps mission goals into the HUD: metrics, thresholds, escalation triggers. Converts "winning" into defined telemetry.
- **Replaces:** the reporting/analytics function that produced the rearview-mirror spreadsheet.
- **Success signals:** every mission goal has a live metric; triggers fire early; nobody argues about the score because the score is defined.

## The Creative Pilot

- **Responsibilities:** crafts Mission Scripts that tell the AI how to speak: High Vibe, the Prompt Ladder, the Behavior of the machine's voice.
- **Replaces:** the "translator" layer between intent and execution — and most of the brief-writing middle management.
- **Success signals:** high IRA per phase (8-10 on the rubric); reframing instead of re-explaining; agents that speak the brand.

## The Risk Spotter

- **Responsibilities:** watches for Hallucination Debt; owns the compliance score on the Privacy HUD; snaps the connection when sensitive patterns are output.
- **Replaces:** the compliance-review bottleneck, automated and live rather than weekly.
- **Success signals:** hallucination bought down before shipping; zero sensitive-pattern releases; debt tally visible on the HUD.

## The Diamond Squad shape

Four specialists, four corners, one Pilot at the center. The shape is deliberate: every specialist maps a human skill to a machine capability. The four roles cover Logic, Ops, Creative, and Risk - the four things that go wrong when a squad scales on AI: wrong metrics, dead feeds, blurry voice, and confident fiction.
