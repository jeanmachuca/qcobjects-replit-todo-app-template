# Scoreboard Playbook

> Source: "Vibe Leading The AI: The Corporate Race Against Machines" (Jean Machuca). Framework concept: high-velocity Laps gated by telemetry, driven by the Scoreboard.

## Think in Laps

Replace 2-week Sprints with Laps (4 hours to 4 days). Every Lap ends in a Pit Stop: Review (did NPCs hit the Vibe?), Refuel (more data or better prompts?), Restart (launch the next Lap immediately). The job is to keep Lap Time decreasing without losing Traction.

## Keeping the IRA in the green

1. **Open the Lap with a Mission Script, not a ticket.** A ticket states a task; a script states a Vibe with Constraints and Acceptance. The script is what the Auditor NPC checks against.
2. **Close the Lap with a Checklist Close.** Named verification, not "does it work?".
3. **Never re-roll a red Lap.** A red Lap means the Vibe was wrong. Rewrite the script, restart the Lap, and log the Circuit Delta cost as data.
4. **Re-reference in long Laps.** Working memory is roughly four files / eight turns. If a constraint was set at the top, restate it once the Lap runs past eight turns.

## Incentivizing Circuit Delta

- Publish Lap Times next to the metric. Shrinkage is the win condition; the leaderboard is the scoreboard.
- Reward shrinking Lap Time **only when Traction stays green.** A Lap that took an hour and shipped hallucinated code is not faster, it is on fire.
- The Pilot's Rule in practice: green telemetry cancels the meeting. When everyone knows the score, nobody needs a meeting to find out the score.

## Gamifying the squad

- Run the Lap leaderboard weekly: fastest green Lap per week gets the squad's recognition slot (no cash needed to move a scoreboard).
- Track "drift kills": a Lap "kill" is a hallucination the Auditor NPC caught before it shipped. Encourage the kill count.

## Escalation without meetings

Every trigger has an owner and an act, decided at calibration time:

- Hallucination Debt red -> Risk Spotter snaps the connection.
- Circuit Delta red -> Logic Navigator audits feeds before next Lap.
- IRA yellow -> Pilot rewrites the Vibe.

The HUD is live. The meeting is dead. Adjust the Vibe, not the calendar.
